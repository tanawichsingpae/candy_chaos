# Project status (2026-10-27)

- The Unity 6000.6.7f1 installation now contains `UnityPackageManager.exe`. The previous incomplete-installation issue has been resolved.
- The project contains the three-chapter campaign with garden, caramel desert, and snow themes; two bonus crystal challenges; endless mode; five selectable heroes; eleven normal enemy types; seven timed elite variants; three bosses; PC and landscape touch controls; distinct marshmallow blast, strawberry jam, and blueberry ice weapons and abilities; upgrades with individual icons; saves; original art; and generated audio.
- All C# files compiled against Unity assemblies with no errors. A clean isolated Unity batch import exited with code 0.
- An isolated Unity Play Mode smoke test verified marshmallow splash damage to a nearby enemy, strawberry jam slowing an enemy, blueberry normal ice shots and Blizzard Shield slowing an enemy, all five abilities, projectile upgrades, art loading, bonus stage flow, five new candy enemy spawns, seven elite variants and their projectile attacks, elite gummy summoning a child, elite jelly splitting into three children, and the cupcake death explosion damaging a nearby vulnerable player. It produced no game-script exception. Unity Editor logged one unrelated `UnityEditor.Search.SearchDatabase` indexing exception on startup.
- The SweetElites sheet was edited to separate the four characters with transparent gutters. Runtime sprite crop coordinates now follow those gutters. An alpha scan found zero opaque pixels on either crop seam, and the isolated Unity Play Mode smoke test loaded all four cropped sprites without an assertion failure.
- Difficulty pacing now introduces enemy types over each chapter, limits elite appearances to 1/2/3 in chapters 1/2/3, pauses normal spawns just before the boss, and reduces boss adds. The crystal bonus has 60 seconds and spawns one enemy at a time. Defeat offers a same-stage retry that restores entrance health, combat stats, and upgrades, including in bonus stages.
- The isolated Unity Play Mode smoke test also verified the chapter-one opening enemy pool, one-elite limit, no boss adds in chapter one, and restoration of chapter and bonus-stage checkpoints. The final C# compile and smoke test passed; Unity still reports its unrelated SearchDatabase indexing exception during startup.
- Audio now has distinct synthesized shot and ability clips for all five heroes, quieter throttled elemental and ricochet accents, and sounds for elite appearance, enemy volleys, jelly splitting, and cupcake blasts. A Unity Play Mode smoke test confirmed five hero shot clips, five ability clips, and four elemental clips have non-silent sample data and the gameplay smoke flow produces no game-script errors.
- Windows and Android player builds and visual QA in a visible Unity Game view remain to be performed. Android requires Android Build Support, OpenJDK, and Android SDK & NDK Tools in Unity Hub.
